Our platform introduces a data-driven approach to measure and map a student’s cognitive abilities across multiple levels of thinking. We move beyond traditional grades to understand true potential.

Moving Beyond Traditional Evaluation

Instead of relying solely on marks or rote-based evaluation, we assess how students progress from basic recall to deeper understanding, practical application, critical analysis, decision-making, and creative problem-solving.

Each activity on the platform—whether solving problems, building circuits, creating CAD designs, or operating drone simulations—is intelligently mapped to specific thinking levels.

Mapping the Levels of Thinking

A comprehensive framework to track higher-order thinking development.

1. Basic Recall

Assessing the ability to retrieve and remember fundamental knowledge and concepts.

2. Deeper Understanding

Measuring how well students grasp the 'why' and 'how' behind the information.

3. Practical Application

Evaluating the use of knowledge in real-world scenarios and hands-on activities.

4. Critical Analysis

Developing the skill to break down complex information and evaluate evidence.

5. Decision-Making

Testing the ability to make informed choices based on data and logical reasoning.

6. Creative Problem-Solving

Empowering students to innovate and create new solutions to modern challenges.
