Who We Are

Teachly is the one-stop learning platform — built on a single, radical belief: that the quality of a student's education should not be determined by the size of their city, the budget of their school, or the income of their family.

We are a team of educators, technologists, and dreamers who looked at India's education landscape and saw a fundamental contradiction. On one side: 250 million students with extraordinary ambition, intelligence, and potential.

On the other: a fragmented system where accessing AI education requires an expensive engineering college, learning drone technology requires a lakhs hardware kit, and having a doubt answered at midnight requires knowing the right person.

We built Teachly to erase that contradiction. Not with more content. Not with another tutoring service. With a complete ecosystem — curriculum, future skills, languages, exam preparation, hobby development, and a 24/7 AI teacher — all in one platform, at one price, available to every learner in India from Class 6 to college and beyond.
