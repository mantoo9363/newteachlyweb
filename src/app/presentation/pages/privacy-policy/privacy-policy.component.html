Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Introduction

Teachly ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our educational platform. Please read this policy carefully.

By accessing or using Teachly, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this policy, please do not use our platform.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

  • Name and Contact Details: When you register, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and other contact information.
  • Academic Information: Class, board (CBSE/ICSE/UP Board), school name, and academic performance data to personalize your learning experience.
  • Payment Information: We collect payment details through secure third-party payment processors. We do not store your complete credit card information.

2.2 Usage Data

  • Learning Activity: Videos watched, simulators used, progress tracking, quiz results, and time spent on different modules.
  • Interaction Data: Questions asked to our AI teacher, support tickets, and feedback provided.
  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers for security and analytics.

2.3 Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience, analyze usage patterns, and personalize content. You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Personalized Learning: To customize curriculum recommendations, track progress, and provide adaptive learning experiences.
  • Customer Support: To respond to your inquiries, provide technical support, and resolve issues.
  • AI Teacher Functionality: To power our AI doubt-resolution feature with context-aware responses.
  • Security & Fraud Prevention: To protect accounts, prevent unauthorized access, and ensure platform security.
  • Analytics & Improvement: To analyze usage patterns, improve features, and develop new educational content.
  • Communications: To send important updates, educational content, and promotional materials (with your consent).

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

  • We DO NOT Sell Your Data: We never sell your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.
  • Service Providers: We share data with trusted third-party services (payment processors, cloud hosting, analytics) only as necessary to provide our services.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or to protect our rights and safety.
  • Business Transfers: In case of merger or acquisition, your data will be transferred with equivalent privacy protections.

5. Data Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmission
  • Secure cloud infrastructure with regular backups
  • Access controls and authentication systems
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Payment data processed through PCI-DSS compliant providers

6. Your Privacy Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access & Review: You can request a copy of your personal data we hold.
  • Correction & Update: You can correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: You can request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal obligations.
  • Portability: You can request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Opt-Out: You can opt-out of marketing communications at any time.

To exercise these rights, contact us at: contact@theteachly.com

7. Children's Privacy

Teachly is designed for students from Class 6 onwards. We take special care to protect children's privacy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. Parents and guardians can review, modify, or request deletion of their child's information.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on our platform and sending you an email notification. Your continued use of Teachly after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
